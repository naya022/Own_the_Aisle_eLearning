Own the Aisle

Help every customer finish the project. A 15-minute, mobile-friendly eLearning that teaches store associates to turn "Where's the stain?" into a finished deck.

⏱ 15 min📱 Mobile-first🎮 Branching scenario + 2 games🤖 AI practice coach👥 Blended: ILT + VILT + job aid✅ Kirkpatrick L1–L4
The performance need

Associates answer the question. Customers need the whole project.

Customers often leave with one item and come back for the rest, or don't come back. Associates are friendly but stop at "It's in aisle 24."

The learner

Frontline, on the floor, short on time

New and tenured associates across departments. Learns on a store device or phone between customers, so every screen is short, visual, and tap-friendly.

The business result

More complete projects, more confident associates

Measured by customer survey "found help" scores, items per project basket, and supervisor observations at 30 days.

Learning objectives

After this course, associates will be able to:

Approach

Greet customers within 10 feet with an open, friendly opener.

Ask

Use at least 2 open questions to uncover the full project.

Advise

Recommend a complete project list, including prep items and tools.

Assist

Walk the customer to the product and offer a helpful service.

The blended solution: more than one course

The Savvy Start showed that a single course wouldn't change floor behavior on its own. Associates work different shifts, learn best by practicing, and need help in the moment. So the eLearning sits inside a 4-part blended path.

1
Instructor-led (ILT) · 10 min

Pre-shift huddle

Supervisors introduce the 4 A's with a quick role-play, using a ready-to-run huddle guide.

2
eLearning · 15 min

Own the Aisle course

Mobile-first, self-paced. Games, AI practice coach, branching scenario and a knowledge check.

3
Virtual (VILT) · 30 min

Live practice lab

Distance learning on Teams for associates across stores and shifts: breakout role-plays with peer feedback.

4
Performance support · ongoing

On the floor

Pocket job aid, plus a supervisor observation checklist and coaching at 30 days.

Where AI sped up the work

Learning analysis

Used generative AI (Copilot / ChatGPT) to sort customer survey comments into themes. SMEs then checked every theme before it shaped the design.

Content development

Drafted scenario dialogue variations and distractor answers with AI. Every line was edited for tone and validated by the Customer Experience SME.

Asset creation

Used AI to draft character art concepts and voiceover scripts, then finished them in Adobe Creative Cloud and Storyline. This cut build time compared with a fully manual build.

Step 3 · Proving it worked

Evaluation plan: Kirkpatrick's four levels

Each level answers a different question, from "Did they like it?" to "Did the business get better?"

LEVEL 1 · REACTION

Did they find it useful?

5-question learner survey right after the course.

LEVEL 2 · LEARNING

Did they learn it?

Same 5-item knowledge check before and after, plus a confidence rating. Pass = 80%.

LEVEL 3 · BEHAVIOR

Do they do it on the floor?

Supervisor observation checklist at 30 days, 3 customer interactions each.

LEVEL 4 · RESULTS

Did the business improve?

Pilot vs. comparison stores: "found help" survey score, items per project basket.

What worked
  • The branching scenario was the top-rated part of the course in open comments.
  • "Ask" improved most, from 48% to 86%. Open questions were the biggest gap going in.
  • Supervisors used the pocket job aid for daily huddles without being asked.
Next iteration (SAM never stops)
  • Add department versions (Garden, Plumbing, Electrical) using the same 4 A's.
  • Add a 2-minute refresher at 30 days for the 19% not yet using all 4 steps.
